FAQ

Common questions

Which businesses is Billing OS for?

Salons, beauty parlours, restaurants, cafés, juice and beverage shops and similar businesses that sell services or prepared items. For shops that need batch, expiry and purchase tracking, see our Inventory Management software.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. It runs as a desktop app on one machine, or as a web app on a server that you can use from desktops, tablets and phones.

What happens if the internet or network goes down?

Billing continues. Bills are saved in an outbox on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns, and a provisional receipt can still be printed.

Does it support GST?

Yes. Each item has a GST rate and can be priced inclusive or exclusive of GST. Receipts show the GST breakup and the GST report is available rate-wise and HSN-wise.

Can I track customer credit (udhaar)?

Yes. Put the balance on a customer's credit ledger with credit-limit checks, receive payments against dues and send WhatsApp reminders.

Can I manage staff salaries?

Yes. Set each employee's pay basis, see a suggested salary for any period and record salary, advance and bonus payments. They count as expenses in your profit.

Which printers are supported?

2-inch (58 mm) and 3-inch (80 mm) thermal receipt printers. Receipts print silently from the desktop app.
