Features

Everything from the first bill to the GST return

POS billing

A counter that keeps up with the queue

Search or scan, take payment, print. Built for the keyboard, with a touch grid for tablets.

  • F1 search, F2 pay, F3 hold, F10 print, Esc clear — plus fullscreen till mode
  • Barcode scans add items; repeat scans increase the quantity
  • Cash, UPI QR, card or credit — or split one bill across them
  • Hold a bill, serve the next customer, resume later
GST tax invoices

Professional GST invoices, Tally-style if you like

Standard Billing for everyday invoices; Advanced Billing adds buyer's order, dispatch and delivery details. CGST + SGST or IGST is decided by the buyer's state.

  • Templates: Normal, Classic, Tally Standard and Tally Professional
  • Real A4 pages; long bills carry forward with "Page X of Y"
  • Payment terms (NET 7 to 90, COD, advance…) with automatic due date
  • 1–4 copies: Original, Duplicate, Triplicate, Extra
  • Back-dated bills for managers, numbered in the right financial year
B2B documents

Quotation to invoice, without retyping

Make quotations, estimates, proforma invoices and sales orders — even for someone who isn't a customer yet — and convert them to an invoice when the deal closes.

  • Quotation, estimate, proforma, sales order, purchase order, GRN
  • Credit and debit notes against the original invoice
  • Payment receipts on half A4 or full A4
  • Your own numbering, e.g. QUO/2026-27/00007
Customers & udhar

Every customer's account, always correct

Sales, payments, returns and credit notes post to a simple ledger. A payment settles the bill you choose first, then the oldest open bills, and keeps anything extra as advance.

  • Ledger: Date · Transaction · Debit · Credit · Balance
  • Numbered payment receipts for every payment
  • Last bills, totals and dues at a glance
  • One-click WhatsApp reminders for due balances
  • Print or download a Tally-style ledger as PDF or Excel
Reports & GST

A dashboard for the day, and Advanced Reports for the accountant.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Billing OS work without internet?

Yes. Billing OS is an offline-first desktop app. Its server runs on your own PC, so billing, invoices, ledgers and reports work without internet.

Can I use it for both counter billing and B2B GST invoices?

Yes. Choose Retail, Business (B2B) or Both in Settings. Retail gives POS and Standard Billing, B2B gives Standard or Advanced Billing, and Both gives all three billing screens as tabs.

Does it prepare GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

Yes. Advanced Reports show GSTR-1 (B2B, B2C, credit notes, rate-wise and HSN) and GSTR-3B (output tax against input tax credit) side by side, with CSV export.

Can I manage stock and purchases?

Yes. Inventory & Stock, Warehouses, Purchases & Vendors and the Barcode label generator are optional modules. Switch them on in Settings → Modules when you need them.

Can several counters or phones use it?

Yes. Several PCs on the shop network can share one database, and phones and tablets can use it over the shop Wi-Fi or install it as an app. It can also run on a cloud server.

Can I control what my staff can do?

Yes. There are four roles — Admin, Manager, Employee and Cashier — with permissions you can change per user, plus login hours. The server enforces them on every request.

How is my data backed up?

One click exports the whole database to a file you can keep on a USB drive or cloud storage, and restore it at any time.
