FAQ

Common questions

Does the software work without internet?

Yes. Inventory Manager is an offline-first desktop application. Billing, stock, purchases, udhar and reports all work without internet. Internet is needed only for updates and licence activation.

Does it create GST invoices?

Yes. It creates GST invoices with CGST, SGST and IGST, prints them on 2-inch or 3-inch thermal or A4 printers, and exports GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B ready reports.

Can I use it on more than one billing counter?

Yes. The Business plan runs on multiple counters connected to the same database, so stock and billing stay in sync.

Is it suitable for pharmacies?

Yes. Batch numbers and expiry dates are tracked for every product, with alerts before stock expires, and expired or damaged stock can be written off with a reason.

Which computer do I need?

Any Windows 10 or 11 PC or laptop. A barcode scanner and a thermal printer are optional but recommended for fast billing.

How is my data backed up?

A one-click backup exports your full database to a file that you can keep on a USB drive or cloud storage and restore at any time.

What happens after the 30-day free trial?

Buy a yearly licence to continue. Your data is kept and everything continues where you left off.
