Features

Everything your store needs,
in one place

Built from scratch for Indian kirana, FMCG and pharmacy stores. Every feature shaped by real retail business needs.

License & Activation
Hardware-fingerprinted, AES-256 encrypted license binding machine and expiry, with a grace period, renewal screen and anti-tamper clock-rollback protection.
POS / Fast Billing
Keyboard-driven billing (F1 search, F2 pay, F10 print, ESC clear), barcode auto-add, hold-cart, and Cash / UPI(QR) / Card / Udhar split payments with GST invoicing.
Inventory & Batch/Expiry
Product catalog with SKU, barcode, HSN and MRP, batch and expiry tracking, and damaged/expired/returned stock adjustments with reasons.
Vendors & Purchases
Purchase entry automatically updates stock and the vendor ledger — always know exactly what you owe every supplier.
Customers / Udhar (Khatabook)
Credit limits, running balances and repayments for every customer, with one-tap WhatsApp reminder links to collect what's owed.
Reports & Analytics
Daily sales & net-profit dashboard, GSTR-1/3B exports for easy filing, and low-stock & expiry alerts so nothing catches you off guard.
Returns & Write-offs
Customer returns automatically restore stock. Write off expired or damaged goods with reasons for accurate profit & loss reporting.
Backup & Thermal Printing
One-click database export and restore — no command line needed. Plus silent ESC/POS & HTML-to-printer support for 2-inch and 3-inch thermal printers.
