FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Does Billing OS work without internet?

Yes. It runs on your computer with a local database, so billing, printing and reports keep working when the internet is down. Internet is only needed for activation, updates and WhatsApp sharing.

Can I make both B2B and B2C invoices?

Yes. Add the buyer's GSTIN for a B2B tax invoice, or bill walk-in customers as B2C. The tax split (CGST/SGST or IGST) is handled automatically and both are included in the GSTR-1 export.

Which computers and printers are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 PCs. 2-inch and 3-inch ESC/POS thermal printers, standard A4/A5 printers, and USB barcode scanners.

Can I use it on my phone?

Billing OS runs on your billing computer. The mobile view lets owners check sales, stock and udhar on the go — ask us about mobile access when you book your demo.

Is my data safe? Who owns it?

You do. Data is stored on your machine (or your own cloud database on the Business plan). One-click backup and restore are built in.

Can I move my data from my current software?

Item masters and opening stock can be imported from Excel/CSV. On the Business plan our team assists with migration.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Choose Standard or Business to continue. Everything you entered during the trial stays.

What happens when my yearly plan ends?

You get renewal reminders inside the app with a grace period. Renew to keep receiving updates and support.
